Figure 7. Consent lifecycle of the current implementation. Storage grant and optional external explanation consent are separate operations. External withdrawal preserves academic data; confirmed storage withdrawal or deletion removes private records and clears shared attribution. Provider records are outside database cascade scope.

Independent endpoints

PUT /consents/storage and PUT /consents/cross-border-explanation.

Compatibility

Legacy PUT /consents remains; DELETE /me/data requires confirmation.

No generated arithmetic

Every displayed number comes from trusted engine metrics.

Figure 8. LINE intake and deadline delivery

SVG / PNG / All 11 figures (PDF) / Editable DFDs (draw.io)

Figure 8. Two independent LINE processing flows. Signed consented webhook work commits before acknowledgement; a consentless reminder stays request-local and sends afterward. A separate GitHub Actions runner rechecks eligible academic targets and consent, persists retry keys before sending, and records outcomes.

Durable acceptance

A successful acknowledgement follows committed consented work.

No pre-consent queue

A consentless reminder is memory-only and may be lost on process exit.

Bounded delivery

Retries are persisted and limited; no universal delivery guarantee is claimed.
